# Service Architecture

# A. Digital Business Systems
## Business Software
CRM, customer/order systems, inventory, HRM, service/RMA, requisition/approval, dashboards, reporting, role-based portals.

## SaaS Implementation
Setup, configuration, onboarding, access, data preparation, testing, troubleshooting, documentation and training.

## AI Automation
Knowledge assistants, customer-support assistants, FAQ automation, document search, workflow integration and AI-assisted support.

## Web & Portals
Company websites, service sites, product catalogues, customer portals, admin panels, lead systems and maintenance.

# B. Managed IT & Technical Support
Remote computer support, routers, Wi-Fi/LAN, IP/DHCP/DNS troubleshooting, printer/software support, SaaS/product support, issue reproduction and technical documentation.

# C. Technical Project Support
Technical research, vendor/product comparison, BOQ assistance, project documentation, progress reports, handover documentation, surveillance/monitoring documentation, smart-monitoring prototypes and structured troubleshooting reports.

**Scope rule:** regulated or safety-critical design/sign-off must be handled by appropriately qualified/licensed professionals where required.

# D. B2B Technology & Project Enablement
Technical product launch support, market research, dealer/channel research, proposal preparation, tender-document organization, quotation/project-costing support, project coordination, product documentation and support-workflow design.

# Additional capabilities
Technical explainer videos, product demos, company-profile presentations and simple business media. Keep these secondary.
